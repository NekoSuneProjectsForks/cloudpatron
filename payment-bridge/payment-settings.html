Payment gateways

Connect your payment providers without editing environment variables. Secret credentials are encrypted at rest on your server and are never sent back to your browser.

Gateway readiness: Heleket is wired to the new checkout and verified callbacks. Stripe remains configured through the original CloudPatron Admin page for legacy recurring billing. Other gateway settings can be saved here, but those providers are not yet connected to payment processing. Never enter blockchain wallet private keys. The encryption key and vault file must both be backed up securely.
